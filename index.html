OUR STORY

Founded on October 31, 2026, Ho Chi Minh City - Local Travel was established from a shared vision: to make the discovery of our city more accessible, meaningful, and inspiring for everyone.

What began as an aspiration among a group of individuals has grown into a commitment to connecting people with places, cultures, and authentic local experiences. We believe that travelling should not be complicated. Everyone deserves access to reliable information and practical guidance that enables them to discover destinations efficiently, confidently, and at their own pace.

As the creators of this platform, we share the same desire to explore, learn, and experience the city alongside our visitors. We are committed to presenting useful, thoughtful, and trustworthy information while celebrating the cultural diversity and everyday beauty of Ho Chi Minh City.

Our story is not simply about discovering places; it is about bringing people closer to the city, to its culture, and to one another.
